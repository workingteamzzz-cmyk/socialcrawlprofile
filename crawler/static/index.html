News Search

Collect news articles for your keywords and period — media articles only. Two engines: Multi-source (Google News, every outlet, media sitemaps & RSS, Bing, GDELT — no browser) or Browser (Google pages, the way a person searches). No API.

Keywords

Each keyword is a separate Google search. Google operators work too, e.g. "tarif listrik" OR TDL.
