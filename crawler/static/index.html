Paid media

Subscribed to a paid outlet? Log in to it in your browser, copy its cookies, and set them with Login on its row. Every article of that outlet is then read with your subscription. Cookies stay on the server and are never shown again; they expire when the outlet logs you out — set them again then.

Check the outlet's subscription terms: many allow personal reading only.

      Safe mode

      Keeps Google and the team's social media accounts from being blocked: a budget of pages per hour and per day for each site, calmer pauses, and when a site shows a block ("Try again later", "temporarily blocked", a checkpoint, Google's 403) it is left alone for a while (Google 6 h, platforms 24 h) and the team is told on Telegram. Schedules that use the accounts or Google's search pages run at most every 6 hours. What the browser could not read is filled with the API, if set up.
